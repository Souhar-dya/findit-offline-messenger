# Acceptable Use Policy — FindIt

**Last updated:** 2026-10-12

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") governs how the User may use the FindIt application ("App"). It is incorporated by reference into the [End User License Agreement](EULA.md). Violating this Policy is a breach of the EULA.

---

## Prohibited Conduct

The User may not use the App to:

1. **Illegal content or conduct** — send or transmit anything unlawful, or use the App to break the law.
2. **Intellectual-property infringement** — send content that infringes another person's copyright, trademark, or other IP rights.
3. **Harassment and abuse** — threaten, harass, defame, bully, or target other users; send hateful or violent content.
4. **Spam** — send unsolicited bulk messages, phishing attempts, or deceptive content.
5. **Security circumvention** — bypass authentication, probe, scan, or breach the App's security or access controls.
6. **Scraping and automated extraction** — scrape, crawl, or harvest data from the App except as expressly permitted.
7. **Reverse engineering** — decompile, disassemble, or reverse-engineer the App, except where applicable law forbids this restriction.
8. **Resource abuse** — overload, disrupt, or interfere with the App or other users (e.g., denial-of-service attacks, excessive load).

## What the Licensor Can and Cannot Do

FindIt is a decentralized, peer-to-peer application. The Licensor does not operate servers, does not store User Content, and cannot access or remove content from another User's device. Once Content is delivered to another User's device, the Licensor has no ability to delete, modify, or retract it.

Because of this architecture, the Licensor's enforcement options are limited to actions within its own control:

- **Can do:** issue updates to the App that change or restrict behavior; refuse to relay Content for specific Users when the Licensor's own device is acting as a relay; report illegal activity to law-enforcement authorities.
- **Cannot do:** revoke a copy a User has already downloaded (the App is openly downloadable and runs only on the User's device); remove Content from another User's device; prevent direct communication between other Users; monitor or censor the content of messages (they are end-to-end encrypted); retract messages already delivered.

By using the App, the User acknowledges these limitations and agrees that the Licensor is not responsible for Content transmitted between other Users. The Licensor cannot and does not moderate, curate, or verify Content exchanged between Users, and has no liability for such Content.
